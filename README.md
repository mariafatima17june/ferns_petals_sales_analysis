# Ferns N Petals – Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard
**Interactive Sales Analysis | Microsoft Excel**

## 1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing sales data from Ferns N Petals (FNP), a leading gifting and flower delivery platform, to develop an interactive Excel dashboard that provides insights into sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product categories, and occasion-based revenue.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support sales monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Analyse overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across different occasions.
- Examine monthly sales patterns and seasonality.
- Identify cities contributing significantly to sales.
- Enable dynamic analysis through interactive date filters and slicers.

## 3. Dashboard & Key Visualisations

The dashboard includes five key visualisations:

| Visualisation | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Revenue by Occasion | Compare sales contribution across occasions |
| Revenue by Product Category | Identify high-performing categories |
| Monthly Revenue Trend | Understand sales patterns and seasonality |
| Top 10 Cities | Identify leading revenue-generating locations |
| Top 5 Products | Highlight best-performing products |

## 4. Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook is organised into four worksheets:

- **Source Data:** Three individual worksheets containing the original datasets.
- **Pivot Analysis:** Consolidated PivotTable analysis supporting the dashboard.
- **Dashboard:** Interactive visualisation layer presenting key sales insights.

The workbook uses PivotTables and interactive slicers to enable dynamic exploration of sales performance.

## 5. Tools & Technical Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Interactive Slicers and Date Filtering
- Data Aggregation and Analysis

## 6. Business Value
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance, helping business stakeholders identify revenue drivers, understand seasonal demand, evaluate product performance, and recognise important geographic and occasion-based sales patterns.

It demonstrates the application of Excel-based data analytics to convert transactional data into actionable business intelligence.
